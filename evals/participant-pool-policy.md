# Halvorsen University, Department of Psychology
# Participant Pool Policy

Effective: Fall Term 2026. Administered by the Participant Pool Coordinator ("the Coordinator").

## 1. Purpose and Scope

a. This policy governs the Department of Psychology Participant Pool ("the Pool"), through which students in participating courses earn research credit and researchers recruit participants.

b. All studies posted to the Pool must hold current approval from the Halvorsen University Institutional Review Board ("IRB"). Where this policy and an IRB requirement conflict, the IRB requirement controls.

## 2. Definitions

a. "In-person study" means a study in which the participant and a researcher are present at the same physical location.

b. "Online study" means a study completed remotely without a researcher present.

c. "Posted duration" means the duration stated in the study posting under Section 8(b).

d. "End-of-term deadline" has the meaning given in Section 14(a).

## 3. Eligibility

a. A student is eligible to participate if the student is enrolled in at least one participating course listed in Section 4(a) during the current term.

b. A student who is 18 years of age or older on the date of a session may sign up for any study for which the student meets the posted eligibility criteria.

c. A student who is under 18 years of age on the date of a session may sign up only for studies marked "Minors Permitted," and only if a signed parent or guardian consent form is on file with the Coordinator. The scheduling system shall determine age from the date of birth in the student's University record.

d. A student under 18 who cannot participate under Section 3(c) shall complete the course requirement through alternative assignments under Section 7.

## 4. Course Credit Requirement

a. Each participating course requires the following number of credits per term:
   i. PSY 101 (Introduction to Psychology): 4.0 credits.
   ii. PSY 205 (Research Methods) and PSY 210 (Social Psychology): 2.0 credits each.

b. In-person studies earn 0.5 credit per 30 minutes of posted duration.

c. Online studies earn 0.25 credit per 30 minutes of posted duration.

d. Credit is calculated from the posted duration, not the time actually spent. A posted duration that is not a multiple of 30 minutes is rounded up to the next 30-minute increment.

e. No more than 50% of a course's credit requirement may be met through online studies. Credit from the prescreening questionnaire (Section 12) does not count toward this limit.

f. A student enrolled in more than one participating course must meet each course's requirement separately. A single credit may be applied to only one course.

## 5. Sign-Up and Cancellation

a. Students sign up for sessions through the Pool scheduling system. Sign-up for a session closes 12 hours before its start time.

b. A student may cancel a session without penalty up to 12 hours before its start time.

c. A cancellation made less than 12 hours before the start time is a late cancellation and is treated as a no-show under Section 6, unless excused under Section 6(d).

d. Online studies have no scheduled start time and are not subject to Sections 5(b) and 5(c).

## 6. No-Shows

a. A no-show occurs when a student (i) does not attend a session for which the student is signed up, (ii) arrives more than 10 minutes after the session's start time, or (iii) makes a late cancellation under Section 5(c).

b. The researcher shall record a no-show in the scheduling system within the period stated in Section 8(c).

c. No-shows are counted per student per term across all courses. Penalties apply as follows:
   i. First no-show: a written warning is sent; no credit penalty.
   ii. Second no-show: the student's credit requirement for the course with the largest remaining requirement increases by 0.5 credit.
   iii. Third and each subsequent no-show: the requirement increases by a further 0.5 credit, and the student's sign-up privileges for in-person studies are suspended for the rest of the term. The student may continue to complete online studies and alternative assignments under Section 7.

d. The Coordinator shall excuse a no-show if the student submits documentation of a medical, family, or comparable emergency within 5 business days after the session. An excused no-show does not count toward Section 6(c).

## 7. Alternative Assignments

a. A student may choose to complete some or all of a course's credit requirement through alternative assignments instead of research participation. The student is not required to give a reason.

b. Each alternative assignment is a written summary of 600 to 800 words of one empirical article selected from the list published by the Coordinator at the start of each term.

c. Each satisfactory alternative assignment equals 0.5 credit, the credit for 30 minutes of in-person participation.

d. Assignments are submitted to the course instructor and graded satisfactory or unsatisfactory within 10 calendar days after submission. An unsatisfactory assignment may be revised and resubmitted once within 7 calendar days after the grade is returned.

e. Alternative assignments must be submitted by the end-of-term deadline.

## 8. Researcher Obligations

a. Researchers shall post each study at least 48 hours before its first session.

b. Each posting shall state: the posted duration; whether the study is in-person or online; the location, if in-person; the credit value under Section 4; eligibility criteria, including any prescreening criteria under Section 12; whether the study offers payment under Section 10; and whether data are anonymous at the time of collection (see Section 11(e)).

c. Within 3 calendar days after each session, the researcher shall either grant credit or record a no-show. If neither has been recorded by 11:59 p.m. on the third day, the scheduling system shall automatically grant full credit.

d. At the end of each session, the researcher shall provide a written debriefing that explains the purpose of the study and gives the researcher's contact information. For online studies, the debriefing shall appear at the end of the study.

## 9. Studies Involving Deception

a. A study may use deception only if the deception is described and approved in the IRB protocol.

b. The posting for a deception study shall include the statement: "This study may not disclose all aspects of its purpose until the end of the session."

c. A full debriefing that discloses the deception shall be given at the end of the session. If the IRB has approved delayed debriefing, the debriefing shall be delivered no later than the end-of-term deadline.

d. After debriefing, the researcher shall inform the participant of the right to withdraw data under Section 11(d).

## 10. Paid Studies

a. A study may offer course credit, payment, or a choice between them. A participant may not receive both credit and payment for the same session.

b. Where a choice is offered, the participant selects credit or payment at sign-up. The selection may not be changed after the session begins.

c. Studies offering payment only do not count toward any course requirement.

## 11. Withdrawal

a. A participant may withdraw from a session at any time without giving a reason.

b. A participant who withdraws from a session receives the full credit for the posted duration under Section 4. Withdrawal is not a no-show.

c. A participant who withdraws from a paid session receives the greater of 50% of the posted payment or the payment prorated by completed 15-minute periods.

d. A participant may request withdrawal of the participant's data by contacting the researcher or the Coordinator within 14 calendar days after the session. The researcher shall delete the data within 10 business days after receiving the request.

e. Data recorded anonymously at the time of collection cannot be identified and therefore cannot be withdrawn under Section 11(d).

f. Withdrawal of data does not affect credit already granted.

## 12. Prescreening Questionnaire

a. The Coordinator administers an online prescreening questionnaire each term, open from the first day of the term through the 21st calendar day of the term.

b. Completing the questionnaire earns 0.25 credit. Participants may skip any item and still receive the credit.

c. Responses are used only to determine eligibility for studies that list prescreening criteria. Researchers receive only an eligible or not-eligible indicator for each student, not the student's responses.

## 13. Privacy and Participation Records

a. Access to participation records is limited as follows:
   i. The student may view the student's complete record.
   ii. The Coordinator may view all records.
   iii. A researcher may view records only for the researcher's own studies.
   iv. A course instructor may view only the total credits applied to the instructor's course by each enrolled student, and not which studies the student completed.

b. Individual prescreening responses may be viewed only by the Coordinator, subject to Section 12(c).

## 14. End-of-Term Deadline and Carry-Over

a. The end-of-term deadline is 11:59 p.m. on the last day of scheduled classes for the term.

b. Students shall assign earned credits to courses by the end-of-term deadline. Any credit not assigned by the deadline is assigned automatically to the course with the largest remaining requirement.

c. Credits do not carry over between terms. Credits earned beyond a course's requirement are forfeited at the end of the term unless the course syllabus offers extra credit, which may not exceed 1.0 credit per course.

## 15. Disputes and Appeals

a. A student who disputes a no-show, a credit decision, or an alternative assignment grade shall first contact the researcher or instructor concerned within 7 calendar days after the decision. The researcher or instructor shall respond within 5 business days.

b. If the dispute is not resolved, the student may appeal in writing to the Coordinator within 10 calendar days after the response under Section 15(a). The Coordinator shall decide within 10 business days.

c. The student may appeal the Coordinator's decision to the Director of Undergraduate Studies within 7 calendar days. The Director's decision is final.

d. Any dispute concerning a term's credits must be raised under Section 15(a) no later than 5 calendar days after the end-of-term deadline.
